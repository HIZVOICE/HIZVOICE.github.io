MediaTek MT6833 · Dimensity 700 Kernels 4.14 · 4.19 · 6.6 Phone-tested, always

Three kernels. One phone.

Xiaomi left camellia on kernel 4.14. This is the road past it.

It started with Voice 4.14 — the stock-generation kernel, tuned with performance packs in two flavours: Voice-A for AOSP, Voice-H for HyperOS and MIUI.

The 6.6 line exists because another maker left a door open. No Xiaomi phone on this chip ever received a 6.6 kernel — but OPPO published its Android 15 kernel source for the same silicon: its Dimensity 6020 phones (the A78 5G, A79 5G and A59 5G) are MT6833 underneath. That source became the base, camellia's own drivers were ported in from Xiaomi's 4.14 tree, and the kernel was built up stage by stage — Stages 1 to 10, each flashed and tested on the real phone, carrying FinaSU + SUSFS root, NoMount, Baseband Guard, Re-Kernel and Droidspaces support in the later stages.

And a from-scratch 4.19 line is in the workshop now — a clean base, raised the same disciplined way: one step at a time, every step proven on the device before the next begins.

House rules

The doctrine every build on this page was made under.

  • Nothing is "working" until it runs on the phone. A clean compile is a hypothesis; the device gives the verdict.
  • One change at a time. If a build breaks, the cause is the one thing that changed.
  • No logs, no fix. Capture the evidence first — guessing in circles wastes days.
  • Never re-lock the bootloader. Never flash another model's preloader. Some mistakes have no way back.
  • Stock clocks are the shipping rule. Experiments live on experiment builds, clearly labelled — never on the daily-driver line.

The kernel story

Three kernel generations for a phone Xiaomi left on 4.14.

Xiaomi shipped camellia with kernel 4.14 and never moved it. The work here started there — the Voice 4.14 line, tuned with performance packs in two flavours: Voice-A for AOSP and Voice-H for HyperOS/MIUI.

The 6.6 line exists because of a door another maker left open. No Xiaomi device on this chip ever got a 6.6 kernel — but OPPO published its Android 15 kernel source for the same silicon (their Dimensity 6020 phones, the A78 5G, A79 5G and A59 5G, are MT6833 underneath). That source became the base; camellia's own drivers were ported in from Xiaomi's 4.14 tree, and the result was built up stage by stage — Stages 1 to 10, each one flashed and tested on the real phone, with FinaSU + SUSFS root, NoMount, Baseband Guard, Re-Kernel and Droidspaces support in the later stages.

A from-scratch 4.19 line is in the workshop now — a clean base, built up the same disciplined way: one step at a time, every step proven on the device before the next begins.

House rules

The doctrine every build on this page was made under.

  • Nothing is "working" until it runs on the phone. A clean compile is a hypothesis; the device gives the verdict.
  • One change at a time. If a build breaks, the cause is the one thing that changed.
  • No logs, no fix. Guessing in circles wastes days — capture the evidence first.
  • Never re-lock the bootloader, never flash another model's preloader. Some mistakes have no way back.
  • Stock clocks are the shipping rule. Experiments happen on experiment builds, clearly labelled, never on the daily driver line.
